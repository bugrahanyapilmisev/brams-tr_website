Solution 02

Equipment Rental

Flexible terms for project-based needs. Capacity without the capital commitment, and an equipment pool that scales with the site programme.

Flexibility

By Term

Short-term intensity windows or long-term cover for the duration of the project. The term follows the programme.

Capacity

A Scalable Pool

Extra capacity at the peaks of the schedule; no fixed cost carried through the slower stretches.

Risk

Access Without Ownership

The right machine without taking on depreciation, maintenance planning or disposal risk.
